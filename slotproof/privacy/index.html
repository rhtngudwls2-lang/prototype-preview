SLOTPROOF / PRIVACY

Privacy

SlotProof processes the selected PDF and CSV in this browser tab. The tool does not upload files, send decoded contents or open payload links. It uses no third-party requests, trackers, cookies or accounts.

What stays on your device?

The chosen files, extracted values and report data live in the tab’s memory until you clear, reload or close it. A CSV you download remains on your device until you remove it. Your browser or OS may keep its own download history.

Advertising

Ads and ad tracking are not enabled in this edition. The tool itself is no-ad. No advertising request may be made until a separately published content page has an approved provider and opt-in controls.
