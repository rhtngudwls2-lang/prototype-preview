How to check a QR print PDF

Start with the digital PDF that will actually be printed. Export approved positions independently from your production PDF. A flat list of values cannot establish the intended positions.

1. Prepare your CSV

Use UTF-8 and the columns page,row,column,value,kind. Coordinates are one-based. Row 1 is at the top of the displayed PDF and column 1 is on the left. Keep leading zeros, whitespace and case. CSV row order does not matter; repeated values are valid in separate slots.

2. Confirm the displayed layout

Pick the final PDF and the approved CSV. Enter rows, columns and page insets; preview the grid, including each PDF page. The grid must describe a regular, equal-cell layout. Crop and rotation follow the rendered page. Different visible sizes or crop bounds in the same grid job require review, not a pass.

3. Read the result

A mismatch is readable but different. Missing means an expected code had no visible ink in its slot or the PDF page is absent. Extra means a readable unexpected code was detected. Unknown is not a pass; inspect it manually. A truly empty approved blank uses kind blank and an empty value. An entirely blank separator uses kind separator with no row, column or value; decorative pages need review. A QR alongside unexplained marks is held for review, not treated as a clean match. Use QR-only white proof areas; logos, stamps, and trim marks in scanned areas can require review. Undeclared slots never silently pass.

Limits

Text only: standard QR symbols carrying numeric, alphanumeric or UTF-8 text. No GS1, Micro QR, structured append, nontext, color certification, print quality grade or padded/irregular slots. PDF 40 MiB, CSV 4 MiB, 80 pages, 600 grid slots, up to 16 rows and 16 columns. Heavily damaged, too small and off-layout codes can require manual verification. CSV report payloads are JSON string literals to retain zeroes and avoid formula execution.
